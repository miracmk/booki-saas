1. Introduction & Application Identity

This Privacy Policy applies to the BooKi online appointment scheduling and business management platform ("BooKi", "Platform", "we", "us", or "our"), designed, owned, and operated by Ki Software, a division of Ki Business Solutions (official website: https://kibusiness.co; software solutions: https://software.kibusiness.co).

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which BooKi collects, accesses, uses, processes, maintains, protects, and discloses personal data and Google user data obtained from users, customers, and business service providers.

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring full compliance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Turkish Law on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK No. 6698), and Google's API Services User Data Policy.

To deliver seamless appointment bookings, customer communication, and calendar management, BooKi collects the following categories of information:

A. Information Provided Directly by Users

  • Account Registration Details: Full name, business trade name, professional title, phone number, email address, physical location or salon address, and encrypted authentication passwords.

B. Technical & Operational Data

  • Device and Access Logs: IP address, browser type, operating system version, referring URLs, request timestamps, and security cookies used strictly for rate-limiting, session maintenance, and intrusion prevention.

When you choose to link your Google Account with BooKi, our application requests access to specific scopes only upon your explicit consent.

4. Prohibited Uses of Google User Data

In strict alignment with Google Cloud Verification standards and Google API Services User Data Policy, BooKi enforces the following strict prohibitions:

  • No Sale of Google Data: We will NEVER sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize Google user data, contact records, or calendar information to third parties, data brokers, or marketing intermediaries under any circumstance.
  • No Advertising or Marketing: Google user data is NEVER used, transferred, or disclosed for serving advertisements, personalized ads, retargeted advertisements, or interest-based marketing campaigns.
  • No Creditworthiness or Financial Scoring: Google user data is never used to assess creditworthiness, lending eligibility, or insurance risks.
  • Strict Limitation of Use: All Google user data is utilized solely and exclusively to deliver, maintain, and enhance user-facing scheduling, calendar synchronization, and booking features requested directly by the user.

🤖 Explicit Affirmation on Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML)

BooKi explicitly affirms that Google Workspace APIs and data obtained from Google APIs are NOT used to develop, build, improve, fine-tune, or train generalized, foundational, or non-personalized artificial intelligence (AI) and/or machine learning (ML) models.

No customer calendar data, attendee identities, or appointment descriptions are ingested into public or private model training pipelines.

5. Data Sharing, Transfer & Third-Party Disclosures

We do not transfer or disclose your Google user data to third parties for purposes other than those specified in this policy. We share data only in the following strictly confined circumstances:

  • Infrastructure Sub-Processors: Secure, enterprise-grade cloud hosting providers (e.g., ISO 27001 / SOC 2 certified data centers) who process data strictly on our behalf under binding data processing agreements that mandate stringent security and confidentiality.
  • Legal Mandates: Where strictly required to comply with a valid court subpoena, warrant, or binding statutory obligation issued by a competent governmental authority.

6. Technical Security & Protection Mechanisms

We implement rigorous technical, organizational, and physical security measures to safeguard Google user data and personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your browser, our application servers, and Google APIs are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with forward secrecy.
  • Encryption at Rest: All stored OAuth tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) as well as sensitive customer personally identifiable information (PII) are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM (Galois/Counter Mode) with secure cryptographic key management.
  • Cryptographic Integrity & Search: Blind indexing using HMAC-SHA256 ensures that searches can be executed without decrypting or exposing sensitive data in clear text.
  • Access Control & Isolation: Multi-tenant isolation ensures data belonging to one business is strictly inaccessible to another. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) protect administrative endpoints.

7. Data Retention & Deletion (User Control & Rights)

Google user data and calendar synchronization tokens are retained only for as long as your BooKi account remains active and the Google integration remains connected.

How to Disconnect Google Integration and Delete Stored Data:

  1. In-App Disconnection: You can disconnect your Google Calendar at any time directly from the BooKi dashboard by navigating to Settings > Integrations > Google Calendar and clicking "Disconnect". This action immediately, permanently, and irrevocably purges your stored OAuth tokens from our database and terminates all calendar synchronization.
  2. Google Account Revocation: You can independently revoke BooKi's access at any moment via your Google Account Security Dashboard at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  3. Complete Account & Data Erasure: To request complete deletion of your BooKi account, appointments, and all associated personal data, submit an erasure request via our online portal or email our Data Protection Team at privacy@kibusiness.co or hello@kibusiness.co. All verified deletion requests are completely executed within 30 days.

  • The right to request the deletion or destruction of your personal data.

  • Hedefli veya İlgi Alanına Dayalı Reklam Yapılmaz: Google kullanıcı verileri kişiselleştirilmiş, yeniden hedeflemeli (retargeting) veya davranışsal reklamcılık amacıyla kullanılamaz ve aktarılamaz.

  • Hizmet Dışı Kullanım Yasaktır: Google kullanıcı verileri yalnızca kullanıcının talep ettiği randevu oluşturma ve takvim eşitlemesi özelliklerini sunmak amacıyla işlenir.

6. Veri Güvenliği ve Koruma Mekanizmaları

Kişisel verilerinizin ve Google kullanıcı verilerinizin güvenliğini sağlamak için üst düzey teknik önlemler alınmıştır:

  • Aktarım Sırasında Şifreleme: Tüm ağ trafiği modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS protokolleri ve güçlü şifreleme anahtarları ile korunur.
  • Depolama Sırasında Şifreleme (At-Rest): Google OAuth belirteçleri (access ve refresh token) ile hassas müşteri kişisel verileri veri tabanında AES-256-GCM (Galois/Counter Mode) algoritmasıyla şifrelenerek saklanır.
  • Kriptografik İndeksleme: Alan bazlı arama indekslemeleri için HMAC-SHA256 kullanılmakta olup ham veriler açık metin olarak tutulmaz.
  • Rol Tabanlı Erişim ve Çoklu Kiracı İzolasyonu: Çok kiracılı mimarimizde (multi-tenancy) her işletmenin verisi diğerlerinden tamamen izole edilmiştir.
