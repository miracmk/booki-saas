BooKi (developed by Ki Software / Ki Business Solutions) values your privacy and data ownership. In compliance with the Meta Platform Terms, European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Art. 17), and Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK Art. 11), users have the right to request the complete deletion of their data collected through Meta Platforms (Facebook Login, Instagram Graph API, or WhatsApp Business API).

1. What Data BooKi Collects from Meta

When you interact with a business using BooKi via Meta platforms or log in via Facebook:

  • Facebook Login: Public profile details (Name, Email address, Facebook User ID).
  • WhatsApp Business API: Phone number and message exchange timestamps used strictly for appointment confirmations, reminders, and customer support.
  • Instagram Direct: Direct message interactions for booking requests routed to the business calendar.

BooKi never sells, rents, or shares your Meta user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers, and does not use this data for automated profiling or AI model training.

2. How to Delete Your Data via Meta (Facebook Settings)

If you connected BooKi via Facebook Login or granted app permissions, you can revoke permissions and initiate an automated data deletion request directly from Facebook:

Step 1: Log in to your Facebook account and navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
Step 2: In the left sidebar, select Apps and Websites (or visit directly: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications).
Step 3: Search for BooKi in your list of active apps.
Step 4: Click Remove next to BooKi.
Step 5: Check the box to "Delete posts, videos or events BooKi posted on your timeline" and confirm by clicking Remove.
Step 6: In the Removed Apps and Websites tab, find BooKi and click Send Request to notify BooKi to erase your user data from our databases.

3. How to Delete Your Data via BooKi Application

If you are an active business subscriber or registered user on BooKi:

Step 1: Log in to your BooKi dashboard at https://bookiapp.kibusiness.co/portal.
Step 2: Go to Settings > Integrations > Meta / WhatsApp.
Step 3: Click "Disconnect & Delete Data".
Step 4: Confirm the prompt. All stored access tokens and webhook associations are immediately and permanently destroyed.

4. Direct Email Deletion Request (Right to Erasure)

You may also directly submit a manual deletion request to our Data Protection Officer at any time. We will process your request and confirm data erasure within 30 days as required by law.

Email: privacy@kibusiness.co (CC: hello@kibusiness.co)

Subject Line: Meta User Data Deletion Request - [Your Name / Phone Number]

Please specify your registered email address or WhatsApp phone number so our team can locate and purge all matching records from our system.

5. Confirmation & Data Deletion Status

When a data deletion callback is executed by Meta, our system generates a unique Confirmation Code and provides a tracking URL. If you received a confirmation code from Meta, you can verify your status at:

BooKi altyapısını kullanan bir işletmeden randevu aldığınızda veya Facebook ile giriş yaptığınızda:

2. Meta (Facebook Ayarları) Üzerinden Veri Silme Prosedürü

BooKi'ye Facebook hesabınız üzerinden yetki verdiyseniz, bu izinleri kaldırarak otomatik veri silme talebi iletebilirsiniz:

1. Adım: Facebook hesabınıza giriş yapın ve Ayarlar ve Gizlilik > Ayarlar sekmesine gidin.
2. Adım: Sol menüden Uygulamalar ve Web Siteleri seçeneğini açın (Doğrudan bağlantı: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications).
3. Adım: Uygulama listenizde BooKi uygulamasını bulun.
4. Adım: BooKi'nin yanındaki Kaldır (Remove) butonuna tıklayın.
5. Adım: İsteğe bağlı olarak "BooKi tarafından zaman tünelinizde paylaşılan gönderileri, videoları veya etkinlikleri sil" onay kutusunu işaretleyin ve Kaldır'a basın.
6. Adım: Kaldırılan Uygulamalar sekmesine geçerek BooKi'yi seçin ve İstek Gönder butonuna basarak BooKi sistemlerine otomatik veri silme bildirimi iletin.
